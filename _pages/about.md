I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, 2024–Present  
  HKUST NLP Group, advised by Prof. Junxian He
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2020–2024 (graduated in June 2024)
- **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University
